Story

I’m Paul Welby, and I’m asking for help to stay housed

I am an honorably discharged, disabled U.S. Navy veteran over the age of 65, and I have worked hard all my life. My profession is front-end application software development, with a focus on front-end UI design using Angular. For the past 10 years, most of my work has been contract-based, and that has left me vulnerable when a contract ends early.

My last job was supposed to last more than a year, but after I completed my role, the company began losing money and cut the contract short at 9 months. I have been unemployed since June 15, 2026. I receive unemployment compensation from the State of California, but it has not been enough to cover my rent, and I have fallen far behind.

To make matters worse, at the same time, I have been dealing with a painful separation after my wife of 24 years developed severe behavioral health issues. During that crisis, she assaulted me, and I experienced this as elder abuse. I was advised to obtain a domestic violence restraining order, and when she did not appear in court, a 5-year restraining order was issued. We are now legally separated, and I am now living alone in a home filled with many reminders of that loss. This has made an already difficult situation even harder to bear.

I received a 60-day notice from my landlord on October 1, 2026, and I have until {{ deadline }} before I am evicted. The funds from this fundraiser, I am hoping, will help me pay my rent arrears so I can stay housed and avoid eviction.

I also hope to move away from my current residence and relocate closer to my brother, my only living family member, so I can spend my remaining years with some peace and stability.

If you are able to give, any amount would help me through this urgent time. If you cannot donate, sharing this fundraiser would also mean a great deal to me. Thank you for taking the time to read my story and for any support you can offer.
